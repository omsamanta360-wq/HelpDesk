# Help Desk - Project Vision

## Problem Statement
People often struggle to find reliable information about doctors, colleges, hospitals, and other services. Information is scattered across multiple platforms, making comparison and decision-making difficult.

## Solution
Help Desk is an all-in-one platform where users can search, explore, review, and compare doctors, colleges, hospitals, and other services in one place.

## Objectives
- Provide verified information
- Enable easy search and filtering
- Allow side-by-side comparison
- Build trust through reviews and ratings
- Help users make informed decisions

## Target Users
- Students
- Patients
- Parents
- General Users

## Future Scope
- AI Recommendations
- Appointment Booking
- Online Consultation
- Career Services
- Finance Services
- Government Services
